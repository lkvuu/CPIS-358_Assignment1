Return Policy

Our return policy ensures a smooth and fair process when bringing the vehicle back to our branch.

  • Location: Vehicles must be returned to the agreed-upon branch location.
  • Fuel Policy: Vehicles are delivered with a full tank and must be returned with a full tank to avoid refueling fees.
  • Cleanliness: The vehicle interior must be kept reasonably clean and free of garbage upon return.
  • Late Returns: A grace period of 30 minutes is allowed; after that, additional hourly charges will apply
